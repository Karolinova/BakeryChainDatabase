# Business Overview

This project is based on a fictional bakery chain. Each store sells freshly prepared baked goods made on-site and serves individual customers.

As the business grows, there is a need for a database system to help manage and organize company data. This would bring several benefits:

- ability to track sales in each store on a regular basis,
- better understanding of which products are most popular among customers,  
- improved control of ingredient stock levels and reduced risk of shortages or excess,  
- easier analysis of sales to reduce waste and unsold products, 
- better long-term planning based on historical sales data,  
- improved employee management by keeping all information in one place.
